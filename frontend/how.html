Where the prices come from

Hundreds of airlines & agenciespublish their fares
Aviasalescompares them for millions of searches
Skywardrefreshes every 2–15 min, adds ML
Yousearch, decide, book

Popular routes are refreshed about every 2 minutes, everything else every 15. Prices are the cheapest real fares people found recently, so the final price on the booking site can differ slightly. Always check before you pay.

The forecast, simply

  • Typical price: what this route usually costs, from real fares.
  • Booking curve: fares are often high far out, lowest a few weeks before, then climb.
  • Season & holidays: Christmas, Chinese New Year, Hari Raya and summer push prices up.
  • Day of week: Fridays and Sundays usually cost more.

It's a well-informed estimate, not a promise. Average error is shown on the dashboard.

Your privacy

Questions

Do you sell tickets?

No. Skyward finds and compares prices, then sends you to the airline or booking site, where you book and pay.

Why is the price a bit different on the booking site?

Fares change constantly. Ours is the cheapest real fare found recently; seats at that price can sell out. Click through to see the live price.

What does "predicted drop" mean?

The model expects that fare to get cheaper before departure, and shows the best day to buy. It's a forecast, not a guarantee, so if the price already suits you, book.

How do Telegram alerts work?

Tick "Also message me on Telegram" when you create an alert. Telegram opens our bot; press Start and you're connected. Send /stop anytime to stop.

Can I see prices in my currency?

Yes. Choose from 160+ currencies at the top of the dashboard; exchange rates update daily.

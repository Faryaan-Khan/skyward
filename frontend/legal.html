Privacy

Skyward has no accounts, no ads and no tracking or analytics.

  • Price alerts (optional). If you set an alert we store the route, date, target price and the email address and/or Telegram chat ID you give us, only to send that alert. Email alerts start only after you confirm by email.
  • Deleting your data. Every alert message has an unsubscribe link, and you can remove an alert from the dashboard at any time. Removing it deletes the stored email address or Telegram ID.
  • Your browser. Your theme, currency and a private token for your alerts are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device except the token, which is sent to manage your own alerts.
  • Searches. Routes you search are counted (without any personal details) to rank "popular routes this week".
  • Technical data. Your IP address is used briefly in memory to rate-limit abuse and may appear in standard server logs kept by our host.
  • Service providers. The site runs on Render, data is stored with Neon (Postgres), emails are sent by the configured email provider and Telegram messages by Telegram. Fonts load from Google Fonts and scripts from jsDelivr, which receive your IP address as part of a normal web request. These providers may process data outside your country.
  • We never sell or share your details for marketing.

Questions, or want your data removed? Open an issue on GitHub (don't post your email address there; say you'd like to be contacted privately).
